# CS cards (control and logic)

Whiteboard: "control (logic) CS: for, break (skip one turn)", with the example "for range [DIGIT 3] do: ____". Transcript 16:58, 18:14 and 19:24 to 21:10.

"This is where most of the mechanics come in" (18:17). "This is the part where we can have fun" (19:51).

## for

- A **repeat card** (20:07).
- Shape: `for range <DIGIT> do <action>` (board).
- Example: **`for range 3 do +2` heals you by 2, three times** (20:37 to 20:42). That's the same as multiplication for a plain number.
- **The action slot can hold another CS card**, not just a number. Nested loops, "double a for" (20:54 to 21:03). This is what makes `for` more than multiplication.

## break

- **Skips the other side's turn.** Board: "break (skip one turn)". Transcript: "skip their turn ... break, or pass" (22:55 to 23:05).
