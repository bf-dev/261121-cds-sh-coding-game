# DIGIT cards

Whiteboard: "DIGIT 0-9". Transcript 16:46 and 17:10 to 17:24.

- Ten values, **0 to 9**.
- **One digit card at a time. Digits don't concatenate**: playing 1 then 9 is not 19. It's simpler for players and simpler to code.
- Digits are the operands. They fill the number slot of a math card (`+ 2`) or the range slot of a `for` card (board: "for range [DIGIT 3] do:").

Open: how many copies of each digit are in the deck, and whether 0 is special (÷0, log 0, `for range 0`).
