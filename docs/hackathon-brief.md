# hackathon brief

From the organisers' briefing, transcript 02:38 to 15:05. These are external rules, not our design choices.

- theme: **"Your campus, your game"**. Build a playable campus exploration game (06:15).
- audience: first-time visitors to the HKU Zhangjiang base, including offer-holder students and their families and friends. The game should make the visit easier, more engaging and fun: find the way around, discover things about the campus, hidden details (06:26 to 07:01).
- genre and visual style are up to us. Pixel-art RPG was only an example (07:05).
- what's judged: details, clear interactions, and our own design that makes the campus recognisable. "Give someone a reason to smile and explore." The AI model is a small part (08:12 to 08:41).
- **deadline: stop working at 4 pm**, then a live demo (07:40).
- **presentation: about 15 minutes** (09:21).
- **assessment: 40% peer review, 60% judges (Alan and Brian), on design, implementation and presentation** (09:31).
- compute: lab-hosted DeepSeek V4.1 Flash, unquantized, unlimited tokens today. Use it on the campus network "HKU Shanghai" through the lab gateway, with your personal API key (03:38, 10:22 to 11:10). Outside models are allowed (08:12).
- team size: four (05:04).
- bonus: at least one team will be picked to present at a large tech expo in mainland China, if they want to (14:12).

**Never commit API keys, gateway IPs or dashboard passwords to this repo.** Put them in a local `.env`, which is gitignored.

People: Alan (lecturer, runs the lab), Brian (lecturer), Tristan (TA), Ling (briefing, agent questions), IT staff, name heard as "Starix" (network questions).
