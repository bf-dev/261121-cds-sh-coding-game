# progression and story

Sources: part 1 24:04 to 26:36, part 2 05:35 and 07:00 to 07:18.

- You play a **student**. Teachers challenge you to check you're good enough (p2 07:00 to 07:18; 25:48 to 26:11).
- **Each building has something special** (24:04).
- **CDS1 is the main fighting ground** (24:22).
- **A key found in CDS1 unlocks Building 5** (24:38).
- **Building 5 has the power-ups** (24:46 to 25:07): gym = stronger, study = new cards, food locker = health. **Red Bull = two turns.** See [cards-power-ups](cards-power-ups.md).
- **The grand hall is the last stage** (25:10 to 25:22). **Leo the HKU lion is the final boss** (p2 05:35 to 05:40).
- The first battle doubles as a **step-by-step tutorial** (p2 05:45).
- Rejected as too much: a portal to battles in the middle of Shanghai (26:13 to 26:27).
