# progression and story

Sources: part 1 24:04 to 26:36, part 2 05:35 and 07:00 to 07:18, **part 4 14:35 to 15:59**.

- You play a **student**. Teachers challenge you to check you're good enough (p2 07:00 to 07:18; 25:48 to 26:11).
- **CDS1 floors are the levels.** Lobby, then floor 1 = level 1, floor 2 = level 2 and so on: **14 levels**. **Higher floors have harder, smarter professors** (p4 14:35 to 15:05). Real floor functions are in `buildings/cds1/README.md` (the 13/F professor offices are an obvious late level).
- **After floor 14 you go to the grand hall and fight the lion**, Leo the HKU lion (p4 15:05 to 15:16; p2 05:35).
- **Beating professors gives you new functions** (p4 15:43 to 15:48).
- More operator/expression space as levels rise (p4 14:17).
- **Building 5 holds the power-ups**: gym, study, food locker, Red Bull (24:46 to 25:07). A key found in CDS1 unlocks it (24:38). How B5 fits with the 14-floor ladder is open.
- The first battle is a **step-by-step tutorial** (p2 05:45).
- Rejected as too much: a portal to battles in the middle of Shanghai (26:13).
