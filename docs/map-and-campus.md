# map and campus

The real places in the game. Whiteboard: "map" is ticked, then "building: CDS1, B5, B6, MT". Aretha's list in the team chat: "Meituan, B5, B6, CDS 1".

Campus: HKU CDS Shanghai, the "HKU Zhangjiang base", Fangchun Road, Pudong. The exact address doesn't matter for the game (p2 06:00).

## buildings

| place | board | sources | role in the game |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CDS1 | floors 1, 2, 6, 13 | lobby and first floor, "copy for other floors" (01:11 to 01:27). **6/F = normal classroom. 13/F = professors' offices, with a different layout, so it needs its own photo** (p2 05:08 to 05:34) | main fighting ground (24:22) |
| B5 (Building 5) | "2/F, stairs, study area", with lines toward B5 (my reading) | second floor, study area, stairs (00:35 to 00:49) | unlocked with a key from CDS1. Power-ups: gym, study (24:38 to 25:07) |

| MT | "MT" | **MT = Meituan** (p2 05:01; Aretha's list). The photos show the outdoor Meituan food-delivery locker wall | "the food locker" (p2 05:03). Food = health power-up (24:46 to 25:07) |
