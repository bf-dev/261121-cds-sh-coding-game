# education: explaining it to non-coders

Transcript 23:14 to 24:15.

- The audience is first-time visitors, including parents who don't code. **"How we explain this to them is the most important part"** (23:14 to 23:26).
- The card idea is liked but will **confuse first-time visitors without a good up-front explanation** (23:29 to 23:37).
- **Present it as a coding education game**, with an explanation and **worked examples** so people know what each card does (23:42 to 24:01).
- The framing: **"the experience of learning to code in CDS1, the same as being a CDS student"** (24:10 to 24:15).

How we'll teach it (tutorial battle, tooltips, a first-room walkthrough) isn't decided. See [open-questions](open-questions.md).
