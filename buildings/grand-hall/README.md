# grand hall (final boss room)

The room where the player fights Leo the HKU lion after CDS1 floor 14 (p4 15:05; p2 05:35). Insung's photos, two iPhone shots taken during an HKU event with a full audience (raw: `resources/photos/2026-11-21-insung-grand-hall/IMG_1208.heic`, `IMG_1220.heic`). **Which building it is in is not on the photos.** The whiteboard puts "Hall" under B6 (board); `outside/` §3.4 guesses the white glass-box auditorium. Treat the building as open, the room itself as confirmed.

## what it is

A raked auditorium, wider than deep, facing one end stage. Not a banqueting hall: fixed theatre seats in rows, no tables. The two photos look at the stage from the middle and from near the back.

## walk (back to front)

1. Enter from the back. A grey carpet aisle runs down the centre between two seating blocks, with a cross aisle partway down.
2. Rows of fixed seats: grey fabric seat and back with a pale grey plastic shell, a fold-down tablet arm on the aisle seats.
3. At the front a timber stage, about knee to waist high, with a short flight of steps at each side and a low dark front edge.
4. Stage left (the audience's left): a lectern, pale timber top on a white body, the **HKU crest** on its front, a gooseneck mic.
5. The back wall of the stage is filled by one **huge LED wall**, roughly 16:5, edge to edge between the side walls, in a thin dark bezel.

## props and details worth drawing

- **Line-array speakers**: tall black stacks of four boxes, hung on a rig from the ceiling, one each side of the stage.
- **Side screens**: smaller portrait LED screens on the side walls near the stage, showing the HKU crest.
- Wall-mounted security cameras, a small control panel beside the stage, green EXIT signs.
- An illuminated stage-front sign on the floor of the stage (event branding during the shoot; leave it out or replace it with the game's own title).
- Camera tripods and photographers in the side aisles (event only, leave out).

## game use

- The LED wall is the natural boss backdrop: the lion can appear on it or step out in front of it, and the battle code (the move and the shield) can render on it as giant text.
- The lectern with the crest is a good place for the narrator or the tutorial voice.
- The event slide in `hall-stage-led-wall-01.jpg` lists HKU's twenty new research institutes and centres (二十所全新研究所及中心). It is real HKU content and could be used for flavour text, but it is not part of the room.
