# B6: Senior Common Room cluster

**Status.** This block of footage is a Senior Common Room suite, a crested dining foyer with a canteen, and an "AI LABS" reception. The SR suite is confirmed as a Senior Common Room by the plaque on its foyer pillar (high confidence), which is the same "SCR" the whiteboard puts in B6. That the SR suite is inside the building the team calls "B6" is **medium-low**: nothing in the footage shows a building number, a lift directory, or an exterior. "B6" is the team's own label, not an official HKU number (only Building 3 and Building 5 are published). The 10:54 "AI LABS" lobby is most likely Building 3 ground floor: the reception desk reads `AI LABS`, official facts give Building 3 as AI Labs, and it is 36 seconds of walking from the CDS1 entrance lobby. The 10:51 foyer carries a banner for `BUILDING 5 & SPORTS COURT ARE NOW OPEN`, which points that level at Building 5. Treat the three clips as **one hospitality cluster that crosses the podium**, not as one proven building.

## 1. Stair foot and switchback stair

**Walk.** Enter at the foot of the stair on a warm walnut wall with magazine racks, the stair rises to the right of the racks. Climb one flight, turn 180 degrees on a half-landing that looks through a tall window into the stairwell void, climb a second flight, arrive at the SR foyer.

**What is there**

- Foot: full-height vertical walnut panelling (approx `#6B4A2E`), two ladder-shaped oak racks holding `HKU BULLETIN` issues (cover art in green, pink and red, stacked three rows each), loose magazines on the lower shelf. A black aluminium-framed glass balustrade with a slim oak handrail runs alongside the first steps. A small black card reader sits on the wall.
- Stair: broad flights, about a dozen risers visible per flight in frame, solid walnut treads and risers (approx `#8A5732` in light, `#5E3A21` in shade), white plaster stringer walls with a painted white skirting that follows the rake. Glass balustrade panels between black square posts, continuous oak handrail.
- Half-landing: a window (approx 1.2 m wide, full height of the landing) looks sideways into the stairwell void over the lower floor. Below is a wood floor with a black skirting band. Three small round black disc wall lights (approx 150 mm) float on the white wall in a diagonal line up the flight.
- Ceiling lights are hidden in a white cove along the stair wall; the light is warm, low, and even. No daylight except at the half-landing.
- Acoustics: hard surfaces (plaster, stone, wood), no carpet and no soft furnishing, so the stair will echo.

**Game use.** A signature vertical space for the SCR level: two flights with a landing that shows a drop behind the glass. Good for a chase up the stair, a shortcut jump over the void, or a checkpoint at the half-landing window. The `HKU BULLETIN` racks are a free interactable: read an issue, get lore.

- Confidence: high on the monogram and on three lines of text; medium on the words themselves, because at 720p only the word shapes resolve. `SENIOR COMMON ROOM` matches the shape, the line count and the team's whiteboard item, so read it as that.

- Painting: a garden bridge in green, with pink flowers and white balustrade, in a thin gold frame.
- Ceiling: flat white with a stepped coffer and warm cove light; a small sprinkler head and a dark detector.
- Light: warm 2700 to 3000 K, all indirect. No windows in the foyer itself.

**Game use.** This pillar is the level's name plate and the natural place for the SCR title card or a faction crest. A plaque close-up is cheap and reads well in pixel art: `SR` over three lines.

## 3. SR dining room

**Walk.** From the foyer, the dining room opens up to the right of the pillar. A long buffet counter runs along the far wall. Round tables sit on a black and white geometric rug. A white panelled planter parapet separates the near floor from a lower seating strip, and windows run down the right side.

**What is there**

- Ceiling: white coffer with a recessed warm cove, two large rectangular chandeliers of stacked crystal rods (roughly 1.2 m wide), plus a smaller crystal pendant to the side.
- Walls: white pilasters, taupe panels, brass two-arm sconces with crystal drops (paired on the pilasters), a large wall-mounted TV on a side wall.
- Furniture: round dark-wood tables (approx 1.2 m) with a central lazy susan, tub chairs in green leather with a studded nailhead trim and a light piping on the back seam (approx `#3E4B35`), small cream side chairs, a few taller stools at the counter.
- Floor: cream marble as in the foyer, with a black and white chevron/diamond rug (approx 4 by 3 m) under the main table group.
- Planter parapet: white panelled boxes with brass reveal lines, planted with ferns, palms and a large philodendron (approx `#2F5D33` foliage against `#F1EEE8` boxes). It forms a low wall around a lower floor area, so the room reads as two levels.
- Windows: floor-to-ceiling, blue-grey daylight, looking at tower silhouettes with vertical silver bands and a bright sky. Sheer grey curtains at one end. A staff member in a dark jacket stands at the glass in one frame, which gives a useful scale reference against the parapet.

**Game use.** This is the SCR level's main hall: a boss arena that is not the grand hall. The planter parapet is cover; the rug is the arena floor pattern; the chandeliers are the light source and can be shot out to darken the room; the buffet counter is a barricade. Green leather chairs and marble read instantly as "expensive, not student".

## 4. SR corridor and private dining rooms

**Walk.** From the dining room, a corridor runs along the double-height stair void, glass balustrade on one side, panelled wall with paintings on the other. At the end, two doors open into private dining rooms.

**What is there**

- Corridor floor: cream marble with a wide dark walnut inlay band running with the walk. Glass balustrade with oak cap rail over the void.
- Walls: taupe panels with white pilasters, two framed artworks (one a dense colourful street scene, one a red-toned figure painting), green emergency exit signs.
- Doors: tall flush doors in pale timber, dark bronze lever handles, in white portal frames.
- Name plaques: each private room has a small dark plate on the wall beside the door, Chinese characters over a Latin name. One reads `孔慶熒` (characters low confidence, could be simplified) over `HUNG HING YING`. A second plaque further down is too blurred to read, so at least two named rooms exist.

A red inverted triangle sign on a pole (traffic or no-entry marker) is visible outside. The terrace and the tree canopy suggest this room is on a low floor, roughly 2/F to 3/F, not high in a tower.
- Light: daylight from the windows plus a warm pendant; the corridor is warm cove light.

**Game use.** Private rooms are perfect save rooms, briefing rooms, or the room where the lion is first seen through glass. The named plaques give the building a donor-room system the level can reuse: `HUNG HING YING` as a room label on the map.

## 6. Dining Area and canteen

**Walk.** Pass the black-framed `Dining Area` sign on its black post. Inside, a warm dark-oak floor with high tables and stools along the glazing, then the space opens into a large canteen with rows of tables.

**Sign text (verbatim)**

- `Dining Area` (serif, title case) over `用餐區` (traditional characters). Note: earlier notes wrote the simplified `用餐区`; the sign is traditional `區`. High confidence.

**What is there**

- First room: dark stained oak floor boards, tall wooden high tables with black metal stools with woven seats, a stainless pedal bin, white walls, no ceiling features except recessed downlights.
- Glazing: full height on two sides, onto a sunken light well with a clipped hedge, a bare young tree, and a glass louvre wall. Daylight floods the room, sun on the hedge.
- Canteen: rows of light oak tables with rattan-backed chairs and black metal frames (approx `#C4A57B` rattan), white folding tables with white shell chairs on the near side, continuous linear pendant lights, a water dispenser, a green exit sign.
- Canteen glazing: a strip of lawn, boxwood hedge, pale paving, a road and dark stone-clad towers beyond. This is a ground-level room facing the campus edge.
- Colours: floor `#9E7C63`, tables `#D8BC94`, white furniture `#E8E6E0`, hedge `#4E7A3C`, sky through glass `#C8D6DE`.

**Game use.** A recovery room and a canteen vendor: food as a heal, the "MT food locker" loop already in the design. The two rooms give a nice step up in scale, small high-table room then a long canteen, good for a crowd of NPCs.

**What is there**

- Desk: curved, white lacquer with a black stone top, the green disc letters along the front face.

- LED wall: roughly 4 m by 2.5 m, framed in black, playing the anniversary film with warm stage lighting.
- Lounge: a white perforated wall carries a circular porthole screen (approx 2.4 m) showing the same film; a large grey leather ring sofa with a stone-look side table sits in front; ceiling of vertical white fins with warm cove light. **Game use.** Reception and identity checkpoint for the research wing: the desk is where a key card is issued. The porthole screen is a natural boss telegraph, and the ring sofa a safe rest spot. The letter discs are an easy puzzle: collect A, I, L, A, B, S.

## Gaps

1. **The grand hall is not in this footage.** It was closed on the day. Nothing here is the hall: not the SR dining room (flat coffered ceiling, marble, small round tables), and not the barrel-vaulted room on Building 5 1/F (`resources/photos/2026-11-21-map-album/103107-b5-1f-hall.jpg`, wood ribs, long communal tables, crest on the end wall). The final boss arena still has no reference frames.
2. **No exterior, no floor number, no directory.** None of the three clips contains a building name, a lift, a floor plan or a window looking at the building's own facade. That is why the building stays unconfirmed.
3. **The walk between clips is unfilmed** (3 min 52 s, then 3 min 07 s). The cluster may cross two or three buildings inside the podium.
4. **Second private room name is illegible**, and the Chinese characters on the HUNG HING YING plaque are not readable at 720p.
5. **The lion is not in any frame.** No lion statue, no hall doors, no stage.
6. **Stair foot context is missing.** The clip starts already at the racks, so we do not see the door or the lift Aretha came from.

Photos the team should take next, in priority order:

1. Exterior of B6 from Fangchun Road, with the entrance, so the block can be identified.
2. Inside the grand hall: wide shot from the entrance, the stage end, the ceiling, the windows.
3. The lift lobby or stair core of the SCR block, with the floor directory visible (this settles the floor and probably the building).
4. The SR door seen from the corridor, plus any notice board or menu at the entrance.
5. The terrace outside the private dining rooms, looking back at the facade.

## Summary

- 30 images, 25 full frames plus 5 sign and lettering zooms. All JPG, 1280 px wide (zooms vary). Folder size `du -sh` 2.5 MB.
- The SR suite is the only Senior Common Room evidence on film: a marble fine-dining floor, a buffet, private dining rooms and an `SR / SENIOR COMMON ROOM` plaque on the foyer pillar.
- The 10:51 foyer is a campus dining level: crested oak shingle wall, green carpet, `Dining Area 用餐區` sign, high tables and a canteen, with a banner for the Building 5 and sports court opening.
- The 10:54 `AI LABS` reception belongs with Building 3 (AI Labs), 36 seconds from the CDS1 lobby.
- No grand hall, no exterior, no floor number appears anywhere in the three clips, and the gaps between clips are unfilmed.

**Verdict.** This is the SCR cluster, and the SR suite is almost certainly the "SCR" the whiteboard assigns to B6 (plaque-confirmed as a Senior Common Room). But the building number is not proven by the footage, and the three clips most likely span more than one building inside the podium: the SR suite first, a Building 5 dining level second, Building 3's AI LABS reception third. For the game, use the SR suite and the stair as the B6 SCR level as the team intended, use the foyer as the pre-boss crest foyer, and do not use any of this as the grand hall.
