# CDS1

Reference pack for the CDS1 building. Everything a level designer or pixel artist needs for this building, without opening the raw videos.

All images in this folder are full-resolution keyframes from the team's own videos and photos, plus a few official web photos. Sources and timestamps are in the image index at the end.

## 1. what CDS1 is

- CDS1 is the team's name for the main School of Computing and Data Science building on the HKU Zhangjiang Base, No. 500 Fangchun Road (芳春路500号), Pudong, Shanghai. Seen: the school name and crest are on the entrance, the lobby wall and every floor sign.
- Official naming on the web: "HKU Zhangjiang Base", academic unit "HKU School of Computing and Data Science (Shanghai)" / "HKU CDS (Shanghai)", Chinese 香港大學計算與數據科學學院（上海）. Source URLs are in the image index.
- The reference map in `docs/art-style.md` reads a construction board as **C = CDS Main Building**, which matches this building. Inferred, but consistent.
- Floors we have footage of: **1/F** (entrance lobby, turnstiles, cafe, lecture theatre), **6/F** (classroom floor, the "normal" floor type), **13/F** (professors' offices and lounge, different fit-out), **14/F** (director and general office, exhibition lounge), plus one upper floor that is not identified (looks like 2/F, see section 7).
- The floor guide puts the building at least 14 storeys. A photo shows `14` over `B3` on a lift panel, so there are basements, probably B1 to B3. Heard, plus seen on the sign; the basement count is not confirmed.
- What we do not have: the ground floor beyond the entrance strip, any floor plan drawing, floors 2 to 5, 7 to 12, the basements, the lecture theatres 1A/1B/2A/2B/3A/3B (only one unidentified 1/F theatre seen from inside).

## 2. exterior

### massing and shape

- A tall tower with a long low wing beside it. The tower reads as a stack of glass boxes with cantilevered corners; the low wing is 4 to 5 storeys with the same glass banding. Seen in `ext-facade-fins-01`, `ext-service-road-twins-01`.
- Two near-identical towers stand face to face across a paved plaza. Narration: "on the other side there is CDS2 that looks nearly identical", "it's shaped like H" (heard, 110510-koki 0:15 to 0:23). The H shape itself is not verifiable from frames.
- A long elevated deck bridges the pair at about 2nd storey level on three large round white columns, with a round or oval void through the deck. Another crew's outside/ folder covers it; timestamps below.

### facade

- Glass curtain wall. Two facade treatments alternate: dark glass with fine vertical mullions, and dense white vertical fins with black glass bands between them. Bronze-brown mullions and dark recessed ground floors on the low wing. Seen.
- Window rhythm: full-height glazing per bay, slim vertical mullions about 1 m apart, a black spandrel band at each floor line. The white fin version reads as pale grey-white stripes with dark slots between.
- Frit band on the glass doors reads `HKU` and `Est. 1911` in white. Seen at the secondary entrance.

### main entrance

- Full-height glass portal under a deep flat canopy on slim steel rods. A revolving door in the middle, glass swing leaves either side. Brushed steel framing, grey stone paving outside.
- Lettering above the canopy, two lines, wide letter spacing: `香港大学` over `计算与数据科学学院` (simplified characters as built). Web and Koki frames both show the school name above the doors; the official web shot uses traditional characters `香港大學計算與數據科學學院`.
- Low clipped box hedges and shrub beds sit tight against the glass on both sides. Small camera on the facade. Seen.
- Materials: grey granite paving with a subtle mottled pattern, brushed stainless door frames, dark grey metal canopy soffit.

### signage

Verbatim, seen:

- `香港大学` / `计算与数据科学学院` (entrance glass band, simplified)
- `香港大學計算與數據科學學院` (official web entrance photo, traditional)
- `SCHOOL OF COMPUTING & DATA SCIENCE` / `The University of Hong Kong` (white monument sign on the lawn, and the 1/F lobby stone wall)
- `HKU` `Est. 1911` (white frit on entrance glass)

Monument sign: a freestanding white slab on a grass island, crest at the left, `SCHOOL OF COMPUTING & DATA SCIENCE` over `The University of Hong Kong`, a smaller Chinese line below (unreadable at this resolution). Curving granite kerb, black planter boxes with tall grasses around it.

### orientation and neighbours

- The site is one block between four roads, read off a civil defence board: 祖冲之路 Zuchongzhi Road north, 集电路 Jidian Road south, 集创路 Jichuang Road west, 芳春路 Fangchun Road east. Seen, high confidence.
- CDS1 faces the entrance driveway and a lawn with the white `HKU` block letters and a cartoon lion mascot. Building 5 (Student Amenity Centre) is north of CDS1 (heard, medium). The delivery lockers are on the east side (heard).
- Around the building: grey granite paving with long painted yellow lane lines and yellow chevrons, black planter boxes with ferns, low white curved benches, clipped hedges, young bare trees with tripod stakes, a white-columned pergola walkway, a sunken oval courtyard with a sweeping stair.

## 3. 1/F

### the walk

Street, through the revolving door, into a white polished lobby. Reception and a round banquette to the right, the entrance glass with five turnstiles ahead, the school name cut into a tan stone wall over the gates. Past the gates: a lift hall with brushed steel doors and wall screens, a big LED timetable on the flank wall, then a corridor with fluted white panels on one side. The corridor opens into the cafe and commons, and a lecture theatre door sits just before it.

### entrance and lobby

- Revolving door: four glass leaves in a brushed steel drum, in the middle of a glass wall. Flanked by hinged glass doors.
- Lobby floor: cream polished stone with a curved inlay border, very reflective. Walls: white fluted or ribbed panels, plus a tan and gold stone feature wall.
- Feature wall: tan stone with a fine square relief pattern, black HKU crest at the top left, `SCHOOL OF COMPUTING & DATA SCIENCE` over `The University of Hong Kong` in black caps, and a full-height doorway under it leading to the turnstiles. Seen in `1f-lobby-stone-wall-01`.
- Reception: a low counter with a patterned back wall of blue and dark discs on a circular motif board. A round banquette with a slatted light-timber base and cream cushions sits in the middle of the space, with grey floor poufs. Seen in `1f-lobby-reception-01`.

### security line

Five stainless pedestrian turnstiles side by side, each with a pedestal face-recognition terminal (screen at about chest height showing the face). Glass balustrade at the side, cream geometric wall panels. Behind them a second set of doors and a bright corridor. Seen.

### lift hall and screens

- Brushed steel lift doors in a row, HITACHI cars rated 21 persons / 1600 kg, mirrored car walls, yellow button plates, pink prohibition pictograms on the doors.
- Wall screens: one shows the current floor as a big numeral with an up arrow, a poster `BUILDING 5 & SPORTS COURT ARE NOW OPEN` / `Your New Space for Sports, Study & Co-Creation` / `7 Days a Week` / `Open Daily (excluding Public Holidays in China)` / `08:00-22:00` / `HKU Zhangjiang Base` with a QR code, and a date strip `SAT 星期六 / 2026-11-21`. Another shows `HKU` `MAIN LIBRARY` `SAT 8:00`.
- Elevator safety certificates and a 电梯信息卡 (elevator information card) are taped on the wall beside the screen. Real clutter worth copying.

### the floor guide (authoritative floor list)

Black text on a white or brushed wall panel beside the lifts, HKU crest above, English only.

```
SCHOOL OF COMPUTING & DATA SCIENCE
The University of Hong Kong

14      DIRECTOR & GENERAL OFFICE / EXHIBITION LOUNGE
13 / 11 PROFESSOR OFFICE
10 / 9  RESEARCH LAB
 7 / 3  TEACHING & LEARNING LAB / LECTURE THEATRE 3A & 3B
        CLASSROOMS / STUDENT LOUNGE & ZONE     (these lines printed in red)
 2      LECTURE THEATRE 2A & 2B / STUDENT LOUNGE & ZONE
 1      LOBBY / LECTURE THEATRE 1A & 1B
```

- The red block has two stacked numerals, `7` over `3`. Confident but not certain (could be 5); the `LECTURE THEATRE 3A & 3B` line supports 3.
- Floors 12, 8, 6 and 4 are not listed. The guide groups functions, it is not a full floor list.
- A **second directory** exists on another floor (clip 08F0) with slightly different wording: `14 EXECUTIVE & GENERAL OFFICE / EXECUTIVE LOUNGE`, `13 PROFESSORS' OFFICE`, `10 RESEARCH LAB`, one row in red. The IMG_3756 one above is the clearer and is authoritative.

### cafe and commons

- A round room under a circular ceiling ring lined with screens. Floor: white tiles with a scatter of red diamond tiles, like confetti. This is one of the most distinctive floors in the whole campus.
- Furniture: a big round light-timber table with chairs, white round cafe tables, cafe chairs in red, white and blue, tall tables with red bar stools, a long white bench along the wall.
- A bank of glass-front drink fridges (Coca-Cola and vending), a water dispenser, a white counter with a sink.
- Windows onto greenery. Seen.

### lecture theatre

- Grey double doors with vertical glazing, blue access-control panel, white notice sheet, two stainless pedal bins labelled `其他垃圾` and `可回收物`.
- Inside: raked rows of grey and tan fixed seats with fold-away writing tablets, pale wood steps up the aisles, a very large black projection screen on the front wall, blue-grey patterned carpet between rows, track spotlights and a black ceiling, daylight windows on one side. Capacity looks around 120 to 150 seats. Estimated.

## 4. 6/F

The classroom floor type. Narration: "the other floors are the same, so just consider that as same for other floors" (heard, medium). The floor guide groups it with floors 3 and 7.

### the walk

Lift opens into a small lobby with a sculpted crest on a timber wall. The lobby opens into a lounge with a high counter, a navy curved sofa and round tables. A corridor runs along the windows with bins and a photocopier. Rooms 602 to 608 line the other side, glass-walled, with etched numbers. At the end the corridor turns into the big teamwork space: circular banquette, timber bleacher steps, then room 611 and the wall of red HKU letters.

### layout and rooms

Directory drawing on the wall (black line art, red pictograms, a monitor icon on a leader line). Verbatim:

```
<-  TOILET  (male, female, wheelchair pictograms)
    602 STORAGE
    603 DISCUSSION ROOM
    604 GYM
    605 TEACHING ASSISTANTS
    606 STORAGE
    607 MEETING ROOM
    608 DISCUSSION ROOM
    609-610 T&L LAB

->  (male, female pictograms)
    601 TUTORIAL ROOM
    TEAMWORK SPACE
    611-613 CLASSROOM
```

- A second printed copy of the same list hangs on the corridor wall further along.
- Door numbers are etched in hollow stencil-outline digits, with a long horizontal rule under them that turns down into a leader line ending in a small oval door-handle doodle. `6f-room-607-sign-01`, `6f-room-608-glass-01`, `6f-gym-604-door-01` show it. This number style is used across the building.
- Doors: tan or pale beige leaves, some with frosted glass panels, black frames, card reader panels beside them, green EXIT lamps above the corridor.

### lounge and lobby

- Small lift lobby: cream wall, a timber-lined niche holding a sculpted metal or stone crest artwork, a small blue wall screen, green EXIT sign, pale timber floor, white exposed-services ceiling with a black LED trough across it.
- Lounge: built-in credenza and shelf niche in a timber wall, long high counter with stools, navy curved sofa around low white round tables, high cafe tables by the window, blue geometric-pattern carpet from the seating zone onward.
- Corridor along the windows: two black wheelie bins and a white multifunction copier standing loose against the wall. Good clutter for a game corridor.

### gym 604

- Glass wall to the corridor, `GYM` pictogram (a figure lifting a barbell) above a small black keycard panel, and `604` in the outline digits.
- Inside: two or three treadmills in a row under the glass, an elliptical, a recumbent bike, a vertical dumbbell rack, a flat-screen TV on the wall, light timber floor.
- The window view is the good part: low-rise villas with orange gabled roofs, greenhouses, fields, high-voltage pylons, and distant tower blocks on the skyline. Not a city canyon.

### meeting room 607

- The number sits on the wall over a horizontal rule, door open. Inside: a whiteboard wall covered in red and blue marker notes, a green acoustic felt panel on the end wall with a wall-mounted display, a long light-timber conference table for about 8, light grey mesh task chairs, papers, stationery, a green pen cup. Seen.

### discussion room 608

- Seen through its glass wall: round light-oak table on a black pedestal, five white moulded chairs on castors, a white chevron or zigzag acoustic panel below the window, blue patterned carpet. Window shows the same farmland, greenhouses and pylons.

### teamwork space

- A big open room on the blue geometric carpet (navy and pale grey-blue interlocking shapes).
- Centre: a large circular banquette, a dark navy drum with a cream seat ring, beige back cushions and beige poufs around it, wrapping a cream cylindrical column. Square columns clad in pale timber.
- One side: tiered timber bleacher steps used as seating, an indoor amphitheatre, with cushions.
- Furniture: white round tables, bucket chairs in white and green, some with timber legs.
- Full-height windows with slim vertical mullions on two sides.
- Wall: large red `HKU` letters mounted on the wall at one end, over a low bench. Seen in `6f-hk-letters-01`.

### classroom 611

- Number in the outline digits beside a keycard panel, door open onto the room.
- Long continuous white-topped tables on white trestle legs in rows, shell chairs in red and green, a beige-wrapped structural column, a window band fully covered by white vertical louvre blinds, a slim mirror or podium board, white ceiling grid with linear lights, a ceiling camera or speaker. Because the blinds are closed, the room reads as a white box.

## 5. 13/F

The premium floor. The floor guide puts professor offices on 13 and 11.

### the walk

Lift opens on a lobby with a raised red `13` over the doors. A lounge with a long bar counter and cream curved sofas. A pantry tucked into a timber box. Three lift doors in a row on the other side, with a printed room notice taped on the wall. A corridor with dark green high-back booth pods. A second lounge with a navy curved sofa around a column. Then corridors that curve around round timber-clad service cores, past closed professor office doors, to gym 1354 at a corner.

### layout

- The plan curves around cylindrical cores, so the corridor reads as a loop rather than a straight double-loaded corridor. Narration at 0:08: meeting rooms left, professors' rooms right (heard).
- Pantry, lounge and lift lobby sit together; the long corridor of office doors runs off it.

### rooms and spaces

- Lift lobby: brushed stainless doors, raised red 3D `13` above them, warm cove light strip in the ceiling, pale stone floor. Seen in `13f-lift-red-13-01`.
- Lounge: timber floor, white exposed-services ceiling with white-painted ducts and conduits, a long high bar counter with stools, cream curved sofas around low black drum side tables, potted plants, a big window band with grey mullions.
- Pantry: a timber box with a herringbone wood-tile splashback, a wavy patterned tile band above the counter, sink, coffee machine, microwave, kettle, white fridge-freezer, long counter with stools. Seen in `13f-pantry-01`.
- Lift bank: three doors in a row, white orb pendant lights, a small printed sheet listing room details in Chinese taped to the wall, green EXIT lamps.
- Green booth pods: dark green high-back upholstered stalls set against the wall, each with a beige bench seat and a small light-oak table, like study or phone booths. Narration counts four (heard); more than one visible.
- Second lounge: a large curved navy sofa around a beige timber-wrapped column, high bar tables with stools along the glazing, white round tables with green and white chairs, fiddle-leaf figs in white pots, cardboard boxes on the floor (keep the boxes, they are a real detail).
- Corridor furniture: boxes and bags on the floor, grey vinyl floor, timber doors both sides, glass doors at junctions.
- Wayfinding: a wall-mounted digital panel showing a photo of the building with a green button (visitor or access panel).
- Gym 1354: `GYM` pictogram and keycard panel outside, glass wall inside, number `1354` in the outline digits. Equipment: two treadmills and an elliptical or stepper under the window with the city view, a second row of machines, a TV on the wall, timber floor. Long afternoon shadows across the floor.

## 6. 14/F

Director and general office plus exhibition lounge. A richer, exhibition-like floor, and the best-lit footage in the pack.

### the walk

Out of the lift (red `14`), through glass doors etched with the crest and the school name. A long corridor with a wall directory. Into a big lounge with a curved timber reception desk, light oak floor, giant blue-grey `CDS` letters on the wall, round white tables and pale chairs. Then the exhibition: cylindrical history drums, a presentation area with a big LED wall and stepped blue and red seating blocks, a timber terrace band with planters, a long high counter.

### what is on the floor

- Lift doors: brushed steel, red raised `14` above. A photo of the same detail shows an etched or engraved HKU crest beside `14` and `B3` on a brushed panel (`14f-lift-crest-photo-01`). B3 is probably the lift or panel code, not a floor. Uncertain.
- Glass doors into the floor: acid-etched crest (shield, lion, open book, `SAPIENTIA ET VIRTUS`) and etched `SCHOOL OF COMPUTING & DATA SCIENCE` / `The University of Hong Kong`, with a dotted frit pattern along the bottom. Seen close up.
- Corridor: pale walls, dark grey ceiling trough, doors both sides, a wall directory panel, green EXIT lamps.
- Lounge and reception: light oak plank floor, curved low reception desk in pale timber with the school name in black on its face, white round tables with pale wood shell chairs, a window band with grey mullions, a black ceiling trough line.
- `CDS` letters: enormous, blue-grey, with a fine vertical stripe texture inside the letter strokes, mounted flat on the white wall. Seen in `14f-cds-letters-01`.
- Exhibition: cylindrical drums wrapped in printed panels, `HKU HISTORY | THE MILESTONES` on a dark band, timeline labels 1912, 1923, 1941-1945, 1961, 1967, 1999, 2012, 2024; a `2nd YEAR @ CDS` panel; an `About CDS` panel; an `UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMMES` drum with `Delta+`.
- Presentation area: a big LED wall (playing a Delta+ talk in the frames), stepped seating blocks in blue-grey with a few red ones, glossy pale stone floor, mirrored reflections, a planter band with benches along the windows.
- Terrace: timber steps up to a planted band with low benches, windows behind.
- Long high counter with stools at the far side. Seen in `14f-lounge-reception-01`.

## 9. recurring details worth putting in the game

- The outline-digit room numbers with the underline and the little door-handle oval. Use one sprite for every room sign.
- Red raised floor numerals inside lift lobbies (`13`, `14`), brushed steel doors, cove light strips.
- `HKU` and `Est. 1911` frit on glass doors, plus a dotted frit band at the bottom.
- Blue geometric carpet on every student space, timber everywhere on the premium floors, grey vinyl in service corridors.
- White exposed-services ceilings with black LED troughs, green EXIT lamps, wall screens showing the floor numeral and a poster.
- Two gyms with the same kit but different views: farmland and pylons on 6/F, city towers on 13/F.
- Etched crest on glass, sculpted crest on stone, printed crest on the directory. Three different crest treatments are all real.
