# Building 5 (B5), HKU CDS Shanghai campus: reference pack for a 2D pixel-art level

Everything a pixel artist or level designer needs to build Building 5 without opening the raw videos. 60 images plus this file. Facts are tagged **seen** (visible in footage), **heard** (narration only) or **inferred** (my reading). Floor numbers are the least certain part; see section 6.

Image name rule: `<floor>-<what>-NN.jpg`. Derived crops are marked in the index. Video frames are 1280x720 (the source clips are 720p); album photos are 1920 wide; web images keep their original aspect.

---

## 1. What Building 5 is

- Official: Building 5 of the HKU Zhangjiang Base, No. 500 Fangchun Road, Pudong, Shanghai. Phase 2, opened 30 Aug 2026 at the "HKU Magic Hour" event. **Heard/official.**
- Function: sports, co-creation and multi-purpose activity building. Contains the University Hall (site of the High Table Dinner tradition), a fitness room, a dance room, a music room, a game/arcade room, self-study rooms, group study space, meeting rooms, a prayer room on 5/F, and a small performance hall. **Official (HKU, hk01, stheadline).**
- Team names: "Student Amenity Centre", with the 1/F hall called the "University Galleria". **Heard** (narration 01:53 to 02:03 in the Koki exterior clip).
- Position: north of CDS1. **Heard** (same clip). The building reads as a dark metal-and-glass box with stacked cantilevered volumes and bronze-brown mullions, matching the rest of the base.
- Opening hours sign seen on a CDS1 lobby screen: `BUILDING 5 & SPORTS COURT ARE NOW OPEN`, `Your New Space for Sports, Study & Co-Creation`, `7 Days a Week`, `Open Daily (excluding Public Holidays in China)`, `08:00-22:00`. **Seen.**
- Game role (team design): unlocked with a key found in CDS1. Holds the power-ups: gym = stronger, study = new cards, food = health, Red Bull = two turns.

The building is unlocked as a reward space, so it should feel calmer and more generous than the CDS1 fighting floors: warm timber, daylight, soft seating, high ceilings.

## 2. Exterior and entrances

**Seen** in Koki's exterior walk (`resources/videos/2026-11-21-koki/110510-outside-cds1-entrance-b5-lockers-gate.mp4`, 01:56 to 02:10) and in `103019` (medium confidence that this is B5):

- Facade: a stack of dark glass boxes with deep cantilevers, slim bronze-brown mullions, a heavier dark recessed base. Some panels are frosted. Above and behind, a taller glass tower with a gold lattice crown.
- Main glass wall (`ext-hku-glass-wall-01.jpg`): tall curtain wall, slim grey mullions, a horizontal frosted white band at door height repeating `HKU` on every leaf and `Est. 1911` on the right leaf. Automatic sliding doors, warm yellow interior with timber-framed columns and a reception counter.
- Secondary entrance (`ext-entrance-canopy-01.jpg`): glass doors under a dark cantilever canopy, warm timber-lined lobby, clipped hedge, grey granite paving.
- Pylon (`ext-entrance-pylon-5-01.jpg`): slim black-and-white pylon on the hedge bed. Reads `5`, then `← 6-7 BUILDING 5`, then `← 1-2 BUILDING 5`, HKU logo at the foot. The meaning of "6-7" and "1-2" is unresolved (levels, or park block numbers).
- Setting: asphalt service road with clipped hedges on both sides, a turnaround loop, concrete bollards, a guard booth and red-and-white barrier arms a short walk away. **Seen** in the same clip.
- Game detail: the pylon "5" is the cleanest exterior landmark. The `Est. 1911` frit band can be repeated on every glass door in the game.
- Exterior palette (approx): dark glass `#565254`, bronze mullion `#3C3A32`, granite `#B0B0AB`, hedge green `#5E7A46`.

## 3. Floors and zones

Floor model used below (see section 6 for confidence):
1/F University Galleria and marble lift lobby. 2/F amenity floor (sports, games, study stair, lounge, chill). 4/F co-creation rooms reached at the top of the study stair. 5/F learning commons. 6/F and above unseen.

### 3.1 1/F University Galleria (the vaulted hall)

Files: `1f-galleria-hall-wide-01.jpg`, `1f-galleria-hall-aisle-01.jpg`, `1f-galleria-end-wall-02.jpg`.

**Seen** (owner confirmed this is B5 1/F, not the grand hall in B6):

- Plan: a long hall of three parallel naves under white plaster barrel vaults, carried on dark walnut arch ribs, repeated in bays down the length.
- Centre aisle: two very long dark walnut communal tables with bentwood chairs with woven cane seats along both sides. The side naves hold the same tables in rows, plus a wider cross row at the back left.
- Floor: pale oak planks, warm honey tone, visible plank joints.
- End wall: dark walnut panelling with a lit circular / rose emblem at the centre, a low raised platform and head table with six chairs between two lit wall niches.
- Lighting: crystal chandeliers with three tiers, one per bay down the centreline. Wall sconces in white and brass. Warm uplights on the arch piers. Two wooden-bladed ceiling fans at some vault crowns. Small green exit signs on the piers.
- Furniture: a green-teal service cabinet on the left wall; a lit exit sign near it.
- Game detail: a refectory you can run down, crest at the far end as a goal marker, side aisles as shortcuts, chandeliers as light sources. Fits a High Table dinner scene and the "food = health" power-up if the team wants a dining spot.
- Palette (measured): plaster `#B5A598`, walnut `#4E342B`, oak floor `#C89373`, chair wood `#8F705A`, white plaster highlights `#D2B49D`.

### 3.2 1/F marble lift lobby

Files: `1f-lobby-arch-portal-01.jpg`, `1f-lobby-oval-table-01.jpg`, `1f-lobby-picture-wall-01.jpg`, `1f-lobby-pillar-05-b2-01.jpg`, `1f-lobby-feature-stair-01.jpg`, `1f-lobby-mind-your-head-01.jpg`, `1f-lobby-directory-panel-01.jpg`.

**Seen:**

- Floor: cream polished stone with a very large inlaid dark-line geometric lattice (interlocking hexagons and diamonds). The pattern tile reads over a metre across.
- Walls: backlit beige marble panels, warm cove strip at the ceiling, 5 or 6 dark framed artworks at eye level, a dome camera.
- One tall oval pedestal table, maroon wooden base, white top, standing alone in the huge room.
- A tall dark walnut arched portal (about 4 m) opens to a darker space on the hall side. **Inferred** that it leads toward the Galleria.
- Lift 1: brushed steel doors, call panel, red fire alarm pull, green exit sign. A white marble pier beside it is engraved `05` over `B2` in silver outline numerals (and a second pier repeats it). "05" is most likely Building 5; "B2" is unexplained (zone, grid or lift bank).
- A tall dark glass panel beside lift 1 carries many small lines of white text (a tenant or floor directory). Not readable at 720p, but a great prop: draw a dense white text block on dark glass.
- Two black floor stands under the stair: `Mind Your Step 请小心台阶` and `Mind Your Head 请小心碰头`, each with a small HKU monogram.
- The feature stair: a wide ribbon of white plaster with a glass balustrade on slim white posts and a walnut handrail, sweeping up one storey, with a warm LED strip under the cheek of the stair.
- Behind the stair: full-height glass to a paved courtyard with clipped hedging and trees, a green exit sign, daylight.
- Palette (measured): marble `#9E9F9C`, marble bright `#BDC6C4`, dark lattice `#424C4E`, glass daylight `#5B7383` / `#70929B`, walnut `#9D8A79`.

### 3.3 2/F amenity floor (main play space)

This is one large open floor reached by the white feature stair. All sub-zones below were recorded as one continuous walk (`103233` then `103514`), so treat them as one connected plan.

**(a) Stair head: mural and grand piano** (`2f-stairhead-mural-piano-01.jpg`, `2f-mural-piano-02.jpg`)
**Seen:** a glossy black concert grand piano, lid up, on a white patterned rug in front of a floor-to-ceiling pop-art mural. The mural is a cartoon Shanghai skyline: pink Oriental Pearl Tower, a yellow looping road, green mountains, a starry dark blue sky, and a small HKU gate drawn into it with `HKU` over `1911`. Mustard, orange and slate armchairs, a white side table. To the left, a wide corridor of black-framed glass rooms. A tall column of stencilled white wayfinding text on the wall.

**(b) Glass corridor and games** (`2f-glass-corridor-01.jpg`, `2f-pool-room-01.jpg`, `2f-pool-room-02.jpg`, `2f-table-tennis-01.jpg`, `2f-table-tennis-02.jpg`)
**Seen:** black-framed glass partitions with frosted `HKU` and `Est. 1911` bands, pale yellow accent walls, small black access-control tablets. Pool room: green-baize table on a dark wood body, pale wood floor, yellow and grey shell chairs, small white round tables, a long pale shelf. Table tennis room: two blue tables, pale grey sports floor with a white court circle, bright yellow accent wall, dark wood slat ceiling with white LED lines in geometric patterns. A brown plate with a controller icon reads `GAME HUB 电玩区`.

**(c) Music and dance wing** (`2f-directory-wall-01.jpg`)
**Seen, verbatim** on a wall:
`← MEN'S SHOWERS 男淋浴间` / `MUSIC ROOM 1 音乐室1` / `DANCE ROOM 舞蹈室`
`→ TOILET 洗手间` (with a pictogram)
A separate plywood directory elsewhere on the floor lists `TOILET 洗手间`, `PIANO ROOM 钢琴房`, `MUSIC ROOM 2 音乐室2`, `MULTIPURPOSE ROOM 多功能室` (last one partly legible).

**(d) Gym** (`2f-gym-interior-01.jpg`, `2f-gym-canal-view-01.jpg`, `2f-gym-corridor-01.jpg`)
**Seen:** a long glass wall onto the corridor, green artificial turf strip, treadmills, a functional trainer, dumbbell racks, benches, cable and squat machines, rubber floor. Ceiling is dark maroon with angular yellow LED lines. Full-height windows on the outward side look over a canal or river, a highway, green banks and a low city skyline. An `Est. 1911` logo wall in pale yellow and blue, plus cartoon fish and building decals on the glass. Palette: ceiling `#22110E`, turf `#65874A` to `#94D56B`, aqua accent `#78B9C2`, warm floor `#BA9370`.

**(e) Back corridors** (`2f-backcorridor-door-notice-01.jpg`, `2f-teal-bench-corridor-01.jpg`, `2f-corridor-closed-01.jpg`)
**Seen:** white or light grey walls with a black skirting shadow gap, light oak doors, brass plates, black access tablets, green exit signs. One door has a paper note reading roughly `此门已坏` (this door is out of order). A black fire hose cabinet. Some corridors end in blue daylight. One corridor has glass rooms with long teal bench cushions. Another has oak doors with a `Closed` note and small Chinese text.

**(f) Auditorium / performance hall** (`2f-auditorium-01.jpg`)
**Seen:** long, narrow room behind glass. Rows of white chairs with olive-green seats and small writing tablets, facing a dark feature wall with one huge black circle cut into it. Red-orange slatted ceiling. Glass wall on one side onto a walkway with plants and blue light. This is the "small performance hall".

**(g) Curving wood path** (`2f-wood-path-01.jpg`)
**Seen:** a wide curving path of pale wood planks laid over grey carpet like a road. White planter boxes with shrubs, freestanding pale plywood screen partitions with black edging, cafe tables and wooden chairs, columns with stencilled white text, exposed black ceiling with a long curving white LED strip, red and burnt-orange accent walls.

**(h) Tiered study stair** (`2f-study-stair-01.jpg`, `2f-study-stair-02.jpg`)
**Seen:** the set piece. A wide tiered timber staircase used as seats, light oak treads, cushions and bolsters in dusty pink, teal, mustard and orange, small white folding lap desks, white steel and glass handrails, planters at the base. Flanked by double-height voids with grey concrete columns and glass balustrades. The stair rises to a crest wall and continues up to the co-creation floor (see 4/F). Open through three levels: base, a mezzanine with white tables, and a glass-walled upper level. Palette: oak `#B99D6F`, terracotta `#9B5C37`, mustard `#A59235`, teal `#6D99A1`.

**(i) Lounge** (`2f-lounge-01.jpg`)
**Seen:** orange and teal armchairs, a green velvet sofa, brown leather seats, red upholstered booth benches, cafe tables, full-height glass to the canal and green banks.

**(j) Chill zone** (`2f-chill-rope-net-01.jpg`, `2f-chill-mural-01.jpg`, `2f-lounge-hammocks-02.jpg`)
**Seen:** a second large street mural (colourful buildings, a pink tower), a raised wooden bench platform, dozens of round poufs in yellow, teal, grey and orange, a rainbow beanbag. Above the mural hangs a web of ropes and nets with colourful plush shapes, swings and a hammock. A mirrored ceiling above the hammocks. High windows and daylight. Palette: mustard `#C2A24C`, burnt orange `#A9622B`, deep red `#662E20`, teal `#6D99A1`.

**(k) Cafe seating** (`2f-cafe-seating-02.jpg`)
**Seen:** pale wood floor, black exposed ceiling with long white LED lines, white round pedestal tables, pale wood wishbone chairs, a big glass wall into a bright green-walled atrium court with a tree, red-wood clad columns, green upholstered booths.

**Power-up mapping for 2/F:** gym = stronger (obvious). Study stair and lounge = new cards. Cafe and vending = Red Bull (two turns). Auditorium = a scripted scene or tutorial beat.

## 6. Gaps and uncertainties

1. **Floor numbers.** The only hard numbers seen are the room bands: `411-414 / 417 / 418-420` at the top of the study stair, and `501 / 503-517 / 518-522 / 523-530 / 531` on the learning-commons floor. There is no "2/F" or "3/F" sign anywhere in the footage. The pylon lists `6-7` and `1-2` but the meaning is unclear. Treat `2f-` in the filenames as "the floor one storey above the Galleria lobby", not a proven floor number.
2. **Galleria floor.** The owner says the vaulted hall is 1/F. The building entrance, hall, lift lobby and feature stair all read as one ground-level sequence, so I have kept 1/F.
3. **Where the amenity floor sits relative to 4/F.** The tiered study stair is the same stair in `103514` and `103735` (identical treads, cushions, lamps, crest wall). `103735` climbs it and arrives at the 4xx rooms. So the stair connects the amenity floor up to the 4/F co-creation level. Whether that is one storey or two is not provable from the clips; the stair sits in a three-level atrium with a mezzanine, so one storey with a mezzanine is my best reading. Consequence: the amenity floor is most likely 3/F, or 2/F if the stair spans two levels. This is the single most uncertain point in the pack.
4. **Correction to earlier notes.** The 4/F wayfinding text was previously read as `6F-676 / 677 / 678`. At full resolution it is `411-414 / 417 / 418-420`. Use the 4xx numbers.
5. **`05 / B2` pillar.** "05" is almost certainly Building 5. "B2" is unexplained; it does not match a daylight floor, so it is probably a zone, grid or lift-bank reference. Do not draw it as Basement 2 without care.
6. **103019 entrance.** Medium confidence that this glass wall with the `HKU` / `Est. 1911` band is B5's main entrance rather than CDS1. It is the clip immediately before the Galleria and lobby.
7. **Porthole window.** Facts describe a round porthole at the end of a back corridor. No frame in this set shows it clearly; the corridor ends I captured are rectangular windows.
8. **GAME HUB plate.** The `GAME HUB 电玩区` plate with a controller icon is described in the facts but is not captured at readable size here.
9. **Prayer room 531.** Label only. Interior not filmed.
10. **Unseen floors.** 6/F and above, the roof, and the sports court are not in the footage.
11. **High Table photo.** `web-high-table-dinner-01.jpg` shows a High Table Dinner at the Shanghai base, but the room cannot be confirmed as B5's University Hall versus B6's grand hall. Use it for table/stage styling only.
12. **Plan geometry.** The sketches are walk-order diagrams, not measured plans. The building is wide and irregular; do not read wall positions or room sizes from them.

## Answer to the open floor question

The white feature stair rises out of the 1/F lobby to the amenity floor, so that floor is most likely **2/F** (one storey up, medium confidence, no 2/F sign was ever seen). The gym, games rooms, auditorium, study stair, lounge and chill zone are all on that one floor: they were filmed as one continuous walk. The tiered study stair is the same stair climbed at the start of the 4/F clip, and it arrives at rooms **411-414, 417, 418-420**, which is a 4xx band, so the study stair links the amenity floor to the 4/F co-creation level. The explicit floor labels we have are therefore 4/F (rooms 411-420) and 5/F (rooms 501-531, prayer room 531), and the building has at least 6 to 7 levels per the entrance pylon. Confidence: 4/F and 5/F high; amenity floor as 2/F medium, with 3/F possible if the study stair spans two storeys; the earlier `6F-676` reading is superseded by `411-420`.
