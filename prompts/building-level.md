# building or floor level

```
Read docs/map-and-campus.md, docs/progression-and-story.md, docs/avatars.md and docs/education.md. Then look at the photos/videos for <building>, <floor> in resources/photos and resources/videos.

Design this place as a game level:
- what the visitor sees (based on the real photos; name real features you can see)
- one real-campus detail worth discovering here (the brief rewards recognisable details, docs/hackathon-brief.md)
- who you battle here and what new card or rule this level introduces
- what you win or unlock
- how it connects to the next place

Only use places that exist in the photos or docs. If something is missing (e.g. we have no photos of CDS1 13/F), say what we need to shoot.
```
