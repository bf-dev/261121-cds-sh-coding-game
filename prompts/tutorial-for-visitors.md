# tutorial for first-time visitors

```
Read docs/education.md, docs/overview.md and docs/cards-*.md.

The players are first-time visitors to HKU CDS Shanghai, many of them parents who have never coded (23:14 to 24:01 in resources/transcripts/2026-11-21-team-meeting.clean.md).

Design the first two minutes of play:
- the opening screen text (under 40 words)
- a scripted first battle, turn by turn, that introduces DIGIT, then + and -, then x, then for, then break, with one sentence of explanation per new card
- how each card's code meaning is shown (e.g. the card `for range 3 do +2` shows `for i in range(3): hp += 2`)

Plain, warm wording. No jargon without an example next to it.
```
