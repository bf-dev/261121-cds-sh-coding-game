## what was decided here

- 20:07 There is a **demo mode** that plays by itself; the final chase in it is **Leo** (20:16 "chase Leo").
- 22:23 The game needs a **time limit** per move.
- 23:18 to 24:04 The **more realistic art looks worse**; the team prefers the less detailed, more pixel-art look.
- 24:34 "We need a **better storyline**", consistent with HKU Shanghai.
- 26:31 to 27:23 The **defence function is customised before the battle starts**, not by dragging blocks mid-battle; it may be too complicated for a visitor.
- 29:50 **Reduce HP**; 32:17 the first floors must be easy and it gets harder as you go up.
- 32:24 **Fewer shields**: long repetitive battles are boring.
- 32:38 to 33:00 The **repeat block is built by tapping numbers**, and the chips inside it are chosen from what you were dealt at the start of the battle.
- 33:27 to 33:45 **Map**: CDS1 vertical on screen, Meituan lockers to its right, a road between them, Fangchun Road behind the lockers.
- 34:32 The **satellite view posted in the team WhatsApp** is the map reference.
- 35:04 to 35:21 Text in **English** (not Chinese beside English), language choice maybe; the **HKU logo must be the official one**, not redrawn.
- 36:11 to 36:25 "**Who would use minus and divide?**" in an attack: you never want to do less damage.
- 36:54 The **final stage is called Grand Hall, not Leo**.
- 37:59 to 40:14 **Building 5 map corrections**: no cafe, study stairs start at 3/F and connect 3/F and 4/F with the sofas under them, the lift must reach 5/F and work from 2/F, 3/F to 5/F are study and meeting rooms.
