## story

> first of all, you're introduced to CDS. You're a new student, or you're a student at CDS.
> AI has taken over CDS and then your job is to help clear out different kind of bots out of the CDS floors.
> What the professors are there to do is they are there to check that you are qualified and competent
> enough in order to fight the bots, and that's why you have to go through every single floor.

> you start in the grand hall and then you get the tutorial in the grand hall or in building six,
> and then after that you navigate over to cds one and then you start playing the first three floors,
> by the third floor you unlock building five. You can get power-ups. Continue battling in CDS one
> and then the goal is to get to the top floor and after you get to the top floor you'll be able to
> enter the grand hall and you can fight the lion.
